Career Guide

Sports Analyst

← Back to Careers

A sports analyst studies sports performance, player statistics, match results, and team strategies. They use data and observations to identify strengths and weaknesses and provide useful insights to coaches, teams, athletes, or sports organizations.

Salary

₹25,000 – ₹1,00,000+ per month, depending on experience, sport, organization, and level of expertise.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Sports Management, Statistics, Data Science, Physical Education, or a related field
  • Knowledge of sports and performance analysis
  • Basic understanding of statistics and data analysis
  • Experience with sports analysis tools is beneficial

Required Skills

  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Knowledge of sports rules and strategies
  • Data analysis and interpretation
  • Observation skills
  • Communication and presentation skills
  • Computer and statistical skills
  • Attention to detail
