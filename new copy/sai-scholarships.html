SAI Support

SAI Scholarships & Athlete Support

← Back to Scholarships

SAI = Sports Authority of India. SAI operates under the Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports and runs several sports-development programmes providing coaching, equipment, boarding and medical support.

Important SAI programmes

  • National Centres of Excellence (NCOE)
  • SAI Training Centres (STC)
  • National Sports Talent Contest (NSTC) — scouts talent (8–14 age group)
  • Extension Centres of STC

SAI support can include

  • Expert coaching
  • Sports equipment
  • Boarding and lodging
  • Sports kits and medical/insurance support
  • Stipends where applicable

Who can benefit?

Eligibility depends on the specific SAI scheme and varies by age group, sport and performance level.
