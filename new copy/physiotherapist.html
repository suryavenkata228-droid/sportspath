Career Guide

Physiotherapist

← Back to Careers

A physiotherapist helps people improve movement, strength, flexibility, and physical function after injuries or physical difficulties. In sports, physiotherapists work with athletes to support injury recovery, rehabilitation, and safe return to activity.

Salary

₹25,000 – ₹80,000+ per month, depending on qualifications, experience, specialization, and workplace.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) or equivalent recognized qualification
  • Registration with the appropriate professional authority, where required
  • Practical clinical training
  • Additional sports physiotherapy training can be beneficial

Required Skills

  • Knowledge of anatomy and human movement
  • Rehabilitation and exercise skills
  • Communication skills
  • Observation and assessment skills
  • Patience and empathy
  • Problem-solving skills
  • Teamwork and professionalism
