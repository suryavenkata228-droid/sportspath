Sport Guide

Cycling

← Back to Sports

Introduction

Cycling is a sport where athletes ride bicycles competitively on roads, tracks, or off-road terrains. It includes events like road cycling, track cycling, mountain biking, and BMX.

Importance

  • Builds strong leg muscles
  • Improves endurance and stamina
  • Enhances cardiovascular health
  • Eco-friendly and widely popular sport
  • Recognized Olympic sport

Eligibility (Age, Height, Weight)

  • Age: 8–40+ years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Lean and fit body preferred
  • Fitness: High stamina and leg strength
  • Training: Professional coaching recommended

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  1. School Level — Start cycling regularly and join local cycling clubs
  2. District Level — Participate in competitions and improve speed and endurance
  3. State Level — Represent district and gain experience
  4. National Level — Compete in national championships and selection camps
  5. International Level — Represent country and compete globally
  6. Olympics / World Championships — Compete at the highest level

Training Plan (Beginner to Advanced)

Beginner

  • Short-distance cycling, basic endurance rides, fitness exercises

Intermediate

  • Long-distance rides, interval training, strength workouts

Advanced

  • High-speed training, hill training, race simulation, recovery sessions

Diet & Nutrition Plan

Foods: Rice, Pasta, Bread, Eggs, Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts

Drinks: Water, Electrolytes, Energy drinks

Avoid: Junk food, Sugary drinks, Smoking, Alcohol

Required Skills

  • Endurance, Speed, Balance, Coordination, Mental strength, Strategy

Equipment Used

  • Bicycle (road/track/MTB/BMX), Helmet, Cycling shoes, Gloves, Protective gear

Competitions

  • Local races, District events, State championships, National championships, International races, Olympics

Government Job Opportunities

  • Army, Police, Railways, Sports Authority of India

Scholarships

Cycling academies, Universities, Government sports schemes

Salary & Income Opportunities

  • Prize money, Sponsorships, Professional racing teams, Coaching

Benefits & Advantages

  • Improves fitness and stamina, Builds strong legs, Low-impact sport, Career opportunities

Career Opportunities After Retirement

  • Cycling coach, Fitness trainer, Team manager, Sports consultant

Success Stories of Cyclists

  • Chris Froome — Tour de France champion
  • Bradley Wiggins — Olympic gold medalist
  • Deborah Herold — Indian cyclist
