Khelo India

Khelo India Scholarships & Programmes

← Back to Scholarships

Khelo India is a national programme focusing on talent identification, athlete development, competitions, academies and grassroots sport.

Programme focus

  • Talent identification and selection
  • Athlete development and coaching
  • Competition exposure and sports infrastructure
  • Women, para-sports and rural/indigenous sports inclusion

Support for athletes

Identified athletes can receive training, coaching, equipment, scientific support and financial assistance; current government descriptions list approximately ₹6.28 lakh per athlete per year in certain frameworks, including a monthly OPA.

Pathway

Talent Identification → Selection → Training → Coaching → Competition → Sports Science → Higher-Level Performance
