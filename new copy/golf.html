Golf Career Guidance

Introduction to Golf

← Back to Sports

Golf is a precision sport where players hit a ball into a series of holes using as few strokes as possible. It is played on large outdoor courses.

Importance

  • Improves focus and patience
  • Enhances mental strength
  • Has low physical injury risk
  • Global professional sport
  • Played in the Olympics

Eligibility

  • Age: 6–50+ years
  • Height: No restriction
  • Weight: Not important
  • Fitness: Basic flexibility and focus
  • Training: Coaching recommended

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Learn grip and swing, basic putting
  • Intermediate: Improve accuracy, course practice
  • Advanced: Shot control, tournament practice, mental training

Diet & Nutrition

  • Balanced diet
  • Stay hydrated
  • Light meals during play
