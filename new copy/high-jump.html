High Jump Guide

Introduction to High Jump

← Back to Sports

High jump is a track and field event where an athlete jumps over a horizontal bar placed at a certain height without knocking it down. It requires technique, flexibility, and explosive power.

Why It Matters

  • Develops strength and flexibility
  • Improves body control and coordination
  • Enhances jumping ability
  • Offers competitive and career opportunities

Types of High Jump Techniques

  • Fosbury Flop: Modern technique (back-first over the bar)
  • Scissor Technique: Beginner method
  • Straddle Technique: Older competitive method

Eligibility

  • Age: 6–35+ years
  • Height: No restriction

Fitness Required

  • Explosive power
  • Flexibility
  • Speed
  • Balance

Player Role

  • Jumper: Athlete focuses on clearing maximum height
  • Requires proper run-up, take-off, body position, and landing

Career Roadmap

  • Beginner → School Competitions → District Level → State Level → National Level → International Competitions → Professional Athlete

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Basic jumping drills, flexibility exercises, short run-up practice
  • Intermediate: Take-off drills, bar clearance practice, speed training
  • Advanced: Full approach jumps, strength & conditioning, competition-level training

Diet & Nutrition

  • Eat: Protein, carbohydrates, fruits and vegetables
  • Hydration: Water, electrolyte drinks
  • Avoid: Junk food, heavy meals before training

Required Skills

  • Explosive power
  • Flexibility
  • Coordination
  • Balance
  • Focus

Equipment

  • High jump bar
  • Landing mat
  • Running spikes
  • Sportswear

Rules Overview

  • Athlete must jump over the bar without knocking it down
  • Each athlete gets limited attempts per height
  • Failure after attempts leads to elimination
  • Highest successful jump wins

Competitions

  • School competitions
  • District & State meets
  • National championships
  • Olympics and international events

Government Job Opportunities

  • Army sports quota
  • Police
  • Railways
  • Sports Authority of India

Income Opportunities

  • Prize money
  • Sponsorships
  • Coaching
  • Sports academies

Benefits & Advantages

  • Improves flexibility and strength
  • Enhances body control
  • Builds confidence
  • Improves athletic performance

Career After Retirement

  • Coach
  • Fitness trainer
  • Sports teacher
  • Athletics mentor

Famous High Jump Athletes
