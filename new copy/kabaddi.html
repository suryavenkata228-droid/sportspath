Sport Guide

Kabaddi

← Back to Sports

Introduction

Kabaddi is a traditional Indian contact team sport played between two teams of 7 players. The objective is for a “raider” to tag opponents and return to their half without getting tackled, all in a single breath. It requires strength, agility, stamina, and strategy.

Importance

  • Improves strength and endurance
  • Builds agility and quick reflexes
  • Enhances teamwork and coordination
  • Develops courage and confidence
  • Strong career opportunities in India (especially with leagues like PKL)

Eligibility (Age, Height, Weight)

  • Age: 10–35 years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Strong and fit body preferred
  • Fitness: High stamina, strength, and flexibility
  • Training: Coaching under experienced kabaddi trainers

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  1. School Level — Learn basic raiding and defending skills; participate in school competitions
  2. District Level — Play district tournaments and improve performance
  3. State Level — Represent district team and gain recognition
  4. National Level — Participate in national championships and attend selection camps
  5. Professional League — Play in Pro Kabaddi League (PKL)
  6. International Level — Represent India in Asian Games, World Cup

Training Plan (Beginner to Advanced)

Beginner

  • Basic raiding practice
  • Fitness exercises
  • Breathing control

Intermediate

  • Advanced tackles
  • Strength training
  • Strategy practice

Advanced

  • Match simulation
  • High-intensity workouts
  • Tactical gameplay
  • Recovery training

Diet & Nutrition Plan

Foods: Milk, Eggs, Chicken, Fish, Rice, Chapati, Fruits, Vegetables, Dry fruits

Drinks: Water, Milk, Coconut water

Avoid: Junk food, Soft drinks, Smoking, Alcohol
