Sport Guide

Taekwondo

← Back to Sports

Introduction

Taekwondo is a Korean martial art and Olympic sport known for its fast and powerful kicking techniques. It combines combat skills with discipline, respect, and self-defense.

Importance

  • Improves flexibility and speed
  • Builds strong leg power
  • Enhances balance and coordination
  • Teaches discipline and respect
  • Recognized Olympic sport

Eligibility (Age, Height, Weight)

  • Age: 5–35+ years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Divided into weight categories
  • Fitness: Speed, flexibility, and strength required
  • Training: Professional coaching recommended

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  1. School Level — Join Taekwondo academy; learn basic kicks and forms (Poomsae)
  2. District Level — Participate in competitions and improve techniques
  3. State Level — Represent district and gain experience
  4. National Level — Compete in national championships and attend selection camps
  5. International Level — Represent country
  6. Olympics / World Championships — Compete globally

Training Plan (Beginner to Advanced)

Beginner

  • Basic kicks (front kick, roundhouse)
  • Stances and balance
  • Flexibility exercises

Intermediate

  • Advanced kicks, Sparring (Kyorugi)
  • Strength training

Advanced

  • High-speed combinations, Competition sparring
  • Strategy training and recovery sessions

Diet & Nutrition Plan

Foods: Milk, Eggs, Chicken, Rice, Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts

Drinks: Water, Electrolytes

Avoid: Junk food, Soft drinks, Smoking, Alcohol

Required Skills

  • Speed
  • Flexibility
  • Balance
  • Coordination
  • Timing
  • Discipline

Equipment Used

  • Dobok (uniform)
  • Belt (rank system)
  • Chest guard (hogu)
  • Headgear, Shin and arm guards

Competitions

  • School competitions
  • District tournaments
  • State championships
  • National championships
  • Asian Games
  • Olympics
