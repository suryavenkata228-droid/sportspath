Career Guide

Sports Scientist

← Back to Careers

A sports scientist studies how the human body performs during physical activity and sports. They use scientific methods to help athletes improve performance, fitness, recovery, and training. They may work with athletes, coaches, sports teams, research institutions, or fitness organizations.

Salary

₹25,000 – ₹1,00,000+ per month, depending on qualifications, experience, specialization, and organization.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Sports Science, Exercise Science, Physical Education, or a related field
  • Master's degree can provide advanced career opportunities
  • Knowledge of exercise physiology, biomechanics, nutrition, or sports psychology
  • Research or practical experience is beneficial

Required Skills

  • Scientific and analytical thinking
  • Knowledge of human performance and exercise
  • Research and data analysis skills
  • Observation and problem-solving
  • Communication skills
  • Knowledge of sports and training methods
  • Attention to detail
