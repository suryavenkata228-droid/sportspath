Powerlifting Career Guidance

Introduction to Powerlifting

← Back to Sports

Powerlifting is a strength sport that focuses on three main lifts: squat, bench press, and deadlift. The goal is to lift the maximum weight possible in each category, while building strength, muscle mass, and mental toughness.

Importance

  • Builds extreme strength
  • Improves muscle mass and power
  • Enhances discipline and focus
  • Develops mental toughness
  • Growing international sport

Eligibility

  • Age: 12–40+ years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Divided into weight categories
  • Fitness: High strength and proper technique
  • Training: Professional guidance recommended

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  • School Level: Start strength training and learn proper lifting techniques
  • District Level: Participate in local competitions and build strength
  • State Level: Represent district and improve performance
  • National Level: Compete in championships and seek team selection
  • International Level: Represent country
  • World Championships: Compete globally

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Learn squat, bench press, deadlift; use light weights and basic strength training
  • Intermediate: Increase weight gradually, focus on technique, follow strength programs
  • Advanced: Maximum lifting training, competition preparation, recovery sessions, periodization training

Diet & Nutrition Plan

  • Foods: Eggs, chicken, fish, milk, rice, dal, fruits, vegetables
  • Drinks: Water, protein shakes (under guidance)
  • Avoid: Junk food, sugary drinks, smoking, alcohol

Required Skills

  • Strength
  • Technique
  • Discipline
  • Mental toughness
  • Consistency
  • Focus

Equipment Used

  • Barbell and weights
  • Powerlifting belt
  • Wrist wraps
  • Knee sleeves
  • Lifting shoes

Competitions

  • District events
  • State championships
  • National championships
  • International events
