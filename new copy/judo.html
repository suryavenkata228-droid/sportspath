Sport Guide

Judo

← Back to Sports

Introduction

Judo is a Japanese martial art and Olympic sport that focuses on throws, grappling, and submissions. The main aim is to use an opponent’s strength against them rather than relying only on power.

Importance

  • Builds strength and flexibility
  • Teaches self-defense techniques
  • Improves balance and coordination
  • Develops discipline and respect
  • Recognized Olympic sport

Eligibility (Age, Height, Weight)

  • Age: 6–35+ years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Divided into weight categories
  • Fitness: Strength, flexibility, and balance required
  • Training: Professional coaching recommended

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  1. School Level — Join judo classes and learn basic techniques
  2. District Level — Participate in competitions and improve skills
  3. State Level — Represent district and gain experience
  4. National Level — Compete in national championships and attend selection camps
  5. International Level — Represent India
  6. Olympics / World Championships — Compete globally

Training Plan (Beginner to Advanced)

Beginner

  • Basic falls (Ukemi)
  • Simple throws
  • Flexibility exercises

Intermediate

  • Throw techniques (Nage-waza)
  • Ground techniques (Ne-waza)
  • Strength training

Advanced

  • Advanced combinations
  • Sparring (Randori)
  • Competition preparation and recovery training

Diet & Nutrition Plan

Foods: Milk, Eggs, Chicken, Rice, Dal, Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts

Drinks: Water, Electrolytes

Avoid: Junk food, Soft drinks, Smoking, Alcohol

Required Skills

  • Balance
  • Technique
  • Strength
  • Flexibility
  • Coordination
  • Mental discipline

Equipment Used

  • Judogi (uniform)
  • Belt (rank system)
  • Mat (Tatami)

Competitions

  • School competitions
  • District tournaments
  • State championships
  • National championships

  • Olympics
