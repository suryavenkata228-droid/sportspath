About us

Sports Career Guidance System

← Back to Home

Sports Career Guidance System is a platform designed to help students and young athletes explore and build a career in sports. It provides information about different sports, career opportunities, training academies, competitions, scholarships, and eligibility requirements.

The system helps users discover suitable sports based on their interests, skills, age, and performance. It also provides information about national and international sports academies, their locations, available sports, training facilities, age groups, and admission details.

The main goal of this system is to connect talented young athletes with the right opportunities and guide them toward a successful career in sports.
