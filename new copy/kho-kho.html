Kho Kho Career Guidance

Introduction to Kho Kho

← Back to Sports

Kho Kho is one of the oldest traditional sports in India and is played between two teams. It is a fast-paced game that requires speed, agility, stamina, teamwork, and quick decision-making. Players chase and tag opponents while following the rules of the game. Kho Kho improves physical fitness and provides opportunities to compete at district, state, national, and international levels.

Importance

  • Builds physical fitness and stamina
  • Improves speed, agility, and reflexes
  • Develops teamwork and discipline
  • Creates career opportunities in sports
  • Represents the country in international competitions

Eligibility

  • Age: 8–35 years (depending on competition category)
  • Height: No restriction
  • Weight: No restriction
  • Fitness: Good physical and mental health
  • Training: Regular coaching from a certified trainer

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  • School Level: Learn basic Kho Kho rules and techniques and participate in school competitions
  • District Level: Compete in district championships and improve speed, agility, and teamwork
  • State Level: Represent your district and win medals to gain recognition
  • National Level: Participate in National Championships and get selected for national camps
  • International Level: Represent India in Asian and World Kho Kho Championships
  • Professional Career: Play in Ultimate Kho Kho League and represent India internationally

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Warm-up, basic running drills, chasing and dodging practice, stretching exercises
  • Intermediate: Sprint practice, agility drills, team coordination, practice matches
  • Advanced: High-intensity fitness sessions, speed and endurance training, tactical game practice, competition preparation

Diet & Nutrition Plan

  • Foods: Rice, chapati, eggs, milk, paneer, chicken, fish, fruits, vegetables, dry fruits
  • Drink: Water, coconut water, fresh fruit juice
  • Avoid: Junk food, soft drinks, excess sugar, alcohol, smoking
