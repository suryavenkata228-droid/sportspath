Rugby Career Guidance

Introduction to Rugby

← Back to Sports

Rugby is a high-intensity team sport played between two teams, where players carry, pass, and kick an oval-shaped ball to score points. It has two main formats: Rugby Union (15 players) and Rugby Sevens (7 players).

Why Rugby Matters

  • Builds strength, speed, and endurance
  • Develops teamwork and leadership
  • Improves tactical thinking
  • Olympic sport (Rugby Sevens)
  • Popular in countries like England, New Zealand, and South Africa

Types of Rugby

  • Rugby Union: 15 players, full match, more strategy
  • Rugby Sevens: 7 players, faster, Olympic format
  • Rugby League: 13 players, different rules, faster pace

Eligibility

  • Age: 8–35+ years
  • Height: Advantage for forwards (tall/strong), backs (fast/agile)
  • Weight: Depends on position
  • Fitness required: Strength for tackling, speed for running, endurance for full match stamina

Player Positions & Roles

  • Forwards: Props, Hooker, Locks – scrums and physical play
  • Backs: Scrum-half, Fly-half, Wings – running and scoring

Career Roadmap

  • School Rugby
  • Local Club
  • District Level
  • State Level
  • National Team Selection
  • International Matches
  • Professional Leagues / Olympics

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Running drills, basic passing, fitness training
  • Intermediate: Strength training, tactical drills, team coordination
  • Advanced: Match simulations, position-specific training, recovery and injury prevention

Diet & Nutrition

  • High-energy foods: Rice, pasta, potatoes
  • Protein: Chicken, eggs, fish, milk
  • Extras: Fruits, vegetables, nuts
  • Hydration: Water and electrolytes
  • Avoid: Junk food, sugary drinks, alcohol

Required Skills

  • Strength and power
  • Speed and agility
  • Teamwork
  • Decision-making
  • Courage and toughness

Equipment

  • Rugby ball
  • Boots (studs)
  • Mouthguard
  • Shoulder pads (optional)

Rules Overview

  • Pass the ball backward only
  • Score by grounding the ball (Try = 5 points)
  • Conversion kick = 2 points
  • Tackling below the shoulder is allowed
  • Continuous gameplay

Competitions

  • School and club tournaments
  • National championships
  • International series
  • Rugby World Cup
  • Olympics (Rugby Sevens)

Income Opportunities

Benefits

Career After Retirement

  • Referee
  • Commentator
  • Sports manager

Famous Rugby Players

  • Richie McCaw – World Cup-winning captain
