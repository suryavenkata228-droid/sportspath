Career Guide

Athlete

← Back to Careers

An athlete is a person who professionally participates in sports and competitions. Athletes train regularly to improve their physical fitness, technical abilities, and performance. They can compete at district, state, national, and international levels.

Salary

₹20,000 – ₹1,00,000+ per month, depending on the sport, experience, achievements, sponsorships, and level of competition.

Qualifications

  • Basic education and good physical fitness
  • Training in a chosen sport
  • Participation in recognized competitions
  • Sports certificates and achievements are beneficial
  • Advanced opportunities may require selection through sports trials

Required Skills

  • Physical fitness and stamina
  • Speed and agility
  • Discipline and dedication
  • Teamwork and communication
  • Concentration and mental strength
  • Sport-specific technical skills
  • Time management
