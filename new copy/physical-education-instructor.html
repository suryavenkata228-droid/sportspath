Career Guide

Physical Education Instructor

← Back to Careers

A Physical Education Instructor teaches students about physical fitness, sports, exercise, and healthy lifestyles. They conduct physical activities, teach basic sports techniques, organize fitness programs, and encourage students to participate in sports and recreational activities.

Salary

₹20,000 – ₹60,000+ per month, depending on qualifications, experience, institution, and job position.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Physical Education (B.P.Ed.) or related field
  • Master's degree in Physical Education can provide additional opportunities
  • Knowledge of sports, exercise, and fitness
  • Teaching or instructor certification as required
  • Practical experience in sports activities

Required Skills

  • Knowledge of physical education and sports
  • Teaching and communication skills
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Physical fitness
  • Organization and planning
  • Patience and discipline
  • Ability to motivate students
