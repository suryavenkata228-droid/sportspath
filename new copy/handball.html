Handball Career Guidance

Introduction to Handball

← Back to Sports

Handball is a fast-paced indoor team sport where players pass, dribble, and throw a ball to score goals in the opponent’s net. Each team has 7 players (6 outfield + 1 goalkeeper).

Why It Matters

  • Improves speed, stamina, and agility
  • Builds teamwork and coordination
  • Enhances reflexes and decision-making
  • Olympic sport with global recognition

Types of Handball

  • Indoor Handball: Standard 7-player game
  • Beach Handball: Played on sand
  • Mini Handball: For beginners and kids

Eligibility

  • Age: 8–35+ years
  • Height: Not required, though taller players help
  • Weight: Fit and agile body
  • Fitness required: Speed, strength, stamina, coordination

Player Positions & Roles

  • Goalkeeper: Defends the goal
  • Wings: Fast attackers
  • Backcourt Players: Powerful shooters
  • Pivot: Plays near the opponent goal

Career Roadmap

  • Beginner
  • School Team
  • District Level

  • Olympics / Pro Career

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Passing and catching, running drills, basic shooting
  • Intermediate: Tactical play, defensive skills, team coordination
  • Advanced: Match simulation, speed and strength training, position-specific practice

Diet & Nutrition

  • Foods: Rice, bread, fruits, vegetables, eggs, chicken
  • Hydration: Water and electrolytes
  • Avoid: Junk food and sugary drinks

Required Skills

  • Speed and agility
  • Throwing power
  • Coordination
  • Teamwork
  • Decision-making

Equipment

  • Handball
  • Sports shoes
  • Jersey
  • Goalpost

Rules Overview

  • 7 players per team
  • Match duration: 60 minutes
  • Players can pass, dribble, or shoot
  • Maximum 3 steps without dribbling
  • Score by throwing the ball into the goal

Competitions
