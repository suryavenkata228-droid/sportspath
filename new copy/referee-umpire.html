Career Guide

Referee / Umpire

← Back to Careers

A referee or umpire is responsible for managing sports matches and ensuring that players follow the official rules. They make decisions during the game, monitor player behavior, handle rule violations, and maintain fairness and discipline throughout the competition.

Salary

₹10,000 – ₹50,000+ per month, depending on the sport, experience, competition level, and number of matches.

Qualifications

  • Basic educational qualification
  • Knowledge of the rules and regulations of a particular sport
  • Completion of recognized referee or umpire training/certification
  • Practical experience in officiating matches
  • Passing required fitness or officiating tests where applicable

Required Skills

  • Excellent knowledge of sports rules
  • Quick decision-making
  • Strong concentration and observation
  • Communication skills
  • Confidence and fairness
  • Physical fitness
  • Discipline and patience
  • Ability to handle pressure
