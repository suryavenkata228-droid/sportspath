Career Guide

Sports Nutritionist

← Back to Careers

A sports nutritionist helps athletes understand how nutrition supports training, performance, recovery, and overall health. They provide evidence-based dietary guidance and help athletes plan balanced meals according to their sport and activity needs.

Salary

₹25,000 – ₹80,000+ per month, depending on qualifications, experience, specialization, and workplace.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Nutrition, Dietetics, Food Science, or a related field
  • Relevant professional registration or certification where required
  • Knowledge of sports nutrition and human nutrition
  • Practical training or internship experience is beneficial

Required Skills

  • Knowledge of nutrition and food science
  • Communication skills
  • Meal planning and organization
  • Analytical and problem-solving skills
  • Understanding of sports and physical activity
  • Counseling and interpersonal skills
  • Attention to detail
