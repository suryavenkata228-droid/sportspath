Baseball Career Guidance

Introduction to Baseball

← Back to Sports

Baseball is a bat-and-ball team sport played between two teams of 9 players. One team bats to score runs, while the other fields and pitches to stop them.

Why It Matters

  • Improves hand-eye coordination
  • Builds teamwork and strategy
  • Popular worldwide, including the USA, Japan, and Korea
  • Offers professional career opportunities

Types of Baseball

  • Professional Baseball: Played in leagues
  • Amateur Baseball: School and college level
  • Little League Baseball: For kids

Eligibility

  • Age: 6–40+ years
  • Height: No restriction
  • Fitness required: Strength, speed, coordination, reaction time

Player Positions & Roles

  • Pitcher: Throws the ball
  • Catcher: Receives pitches
  • Infielders: Near bases
  • Outfielders: Cover a large field area

Career Roadmap

  • Beginner
  • School Team
  • Club Level
  • State Level
  • National Team
  • International Matches
  • Professional League

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Throwing and catching, batting basics
  • Intermediate: Fielding drills, game strategies
  • Advanced: Match practice, strength and conditioning

Diet & Nutrition

  • Protein: Eggs, chicken
  • Carbohydrates: Rice, bread
  • Fruits
  • Hydration: Water and sports drinks
  • Avoid: Junk food and excess sugar

Required Skills

  • Batting power
  • Throwing accuracy
  • Fielding skills
  • Speed and agility
  • Focus and decision-making

Equipment

  • Bat
  • Ball
  • Glove
  • Helmet
  • Cleats

Rules Overview

  • 9 players per team
  • Game divided into 9 innings
  • Pitcher throws the ball to the batter
  • Batter hits and runs bases
  • Score by completing all bases

Competitions

  • School tournaments
  • National championships
  • International competitions
  • Major League Baseball
