Private Sports Scholarships

← Back to Scholarships

Private sports scholarships are provided by companies, foundations, NGOs, sports organisations and philanthropic institutions. They often offer additional support such as coaching, sports science, nutrition, equipment, competition exposure and mentoring.

Examples

  • Reliance Foundation — athlete support across multiple Olympic sports and development programmes.
  • Olympic Gold Quest — supports athletes with training, international competition exposure and sports science.
  • Tata Trusts — regional sports development and athlete-support initiatives.

Benefits

  • Professional coaching and training
  • Sports science and medical support
  • Equipment and facilities
  • Competition exposure and mentoring
  • Education support

Limitations

  • Selection is competitive and limited.
  • Programmes may focus on specific sports.
  • Application periods and eligibility vary by provider.
