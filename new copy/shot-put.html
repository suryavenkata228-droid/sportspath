Shot Put Guide

Introduction to Shot Put

← Back to Sports

Shot put is a track and field event where an athlete throws (puts) a heavy spherical ball called a “shot” as far as possible using one hand. It focuses on strength, technique, and explosive power.

Why It Matters

  • Builds muscular strength
  • Improves coordination and balance
  • Develops explosive power
  • Provides career opportunities in sports

Types of Shot Put Techniques

  • Glide Technique: Athlete moves linearly across the circle before throwing
  • Spin (Rotational) Technique: Athlete rotates like discus throw for more momentum

Eligibility

  • Age: 6 years and above
  • Gender: Both male and female

Fitness Required

  • Upper body strength
  • Core strength
  • Balance
  • Coordination

Player Role

  • Thrower (Putter): Athlete aims to throw the shot to the maximum possible distance using proper technique

Career Roadmap

  • Beginner → School Level → District Level → State Level → National Level → International Competitions → Professional Athlete

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Basic throwing techniques, light strength training, balance exercises
  • Intermediate: Glide or spin technique practice, strength and conditioning, footwork drills
  • Advanced: Power training (weights), advanced technique improvement, competition-level practice

Diet & Nutrition

  • Eat: Protein, carbohydrates, fruits and vegetables
  • Hydration: Plenty of water, electrolyte drinks
  • Avoid: Junk food, oily and heavy meals before training

Required Skills

  • Strength
  • Explosive power
  • Coordination
  • Balance
  • Focus

Equipment

  • Shot (metal ball)
  • Throwing circle
  • Toe board
  • Sports shoes

Rules Overview

  • Shot must be thrown using one hand from the shoulder
  • Athlete must stay inside the circle
  • Throw must land within the marked sector
  • Longest valid throw wins

Competitions

  • School competitions
  • District & State meets
  • National championships
  • Olympics and international events

Government Job Opportunities

  • Army sports quota
  • Police department
  • Railways
  • Sports Authority of India

Income Opportunities

  • Prize money
  • Sponsorships
  • Coaching career
  • Sports academies

Benefits & Advantages

  • Increases body strength
  • Improves coordination
  • Enhances athletic performance
  • Builds confidence

Career After Retirement

  • Coach
  • Fitness trainer
  • Physical education teacher
  • Sports mentor
