Archery

Introduction

Archery is a sport where athletes use a bow to shoot arrows at a target. The goal is to hit the center (bullseye) from a fixed distance with maximum accuracy.

Importance

  • Improves focus and concentration
  • Enhances hand-eye coordination
  • Builds patience and mental control
  • Develops precision skills
  • Recognized Olympic sport

Eligibility (Age, Height, Weight)

  • Age: 8–40+ years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Normal and fit body
  • Fitness: Good upper body strength and focus
  • Training: Professional coaching recommended

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  1. School Level — Join archery academy and learn basic shooting techniques
  2. District Level — Participate in local competitions and improve accuracy
  3. State Level — Represent district and gain experience
  4. National Level — Compete in national championships and selection camps
  5. International Level — Represent country in major events
  6. Olympics / World Championships — Compete globally

Training Plan (Beginner to Advanced)

Beginner

  • Basic bow handling, target practice (short distance), body posture training

Intermediate

  • Accuracy training, distance shooting, strength exercises

Advanced

  • Precision shooting, competition practice, mental focus training, recovery sessions

Diet & Nutrition Plan

Foods: Milk, Eggs, Fruits, Vegetables, Nuts, Rice

Drinks: Water, Electrolytes

Avoid: Junk food, Soft drinks, Smoking, Alcohol

Required Skills

  • Concentration, Accuracy, Hand-eye coordination, Patience, Mental strength, Stability

Equipment Used

  • Bow (recurve/compound), Arrows, Target board, Finger tab, Arm guard

Competitions

  • School competitions, District tournaments, State championships, National championships, International events, Olympics

Government Job Opportunities

  • Army, Police, Railways, Sports Authority of India

Scholarships

Sports academies, Universities, Government schemes

Salary & Income Opportunities

  • Prize money, Sponsorships, Government jobs, Coaching

Benefits & Advantages

  • Improves focus, Builds patience, Low physical injury risk, Career opportunities, International recognition

Career Opportunities After Retirement

  • Archery coach, Trainer, Sports official, Academy owner

Success Stories of Archers

  • Deepika Kumari — World-class archer
  • Atanu Das — Indian Olympian
  • Kim Woo-jin — Olympic champion
