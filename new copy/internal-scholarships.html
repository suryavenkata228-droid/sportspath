Institutional Support

Internal / Institutional Sports Scholarships

← Back to Scholarships

Internal scholarships are offered directly by schools, colleges, universities and sports academies as fee concessions, free training, hostel support or travel assistance based on sporting achievements.

Common benefits

  • Tuition-fee concession
  • Free/discounted hostel
  • Free sports training and equipment
  • Travel support and competition fees
  • Academic support and examination concessions

Eligibility and documents

Eligibility varies by institution and usually requires sports certificates, academic records and recommendation from a sports committee.
