Career Guide

Sports Teacher

← Back to Careers

A sports teacher teaches students different sports and physical activities in schools and colleges. They help students develop fitness, teamwork, discipline, coordination, and sports skills. They also organize sports events and encourage students to participate in competitions.

Salary

₹20,000 – ₹60,000+ per month, depending on qualifications, experience, school, and organization.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Physical Education (B.P.Ed.) or related field
  • Master's degree in Physical Education can provide additional opportunities
  • Knowledge of different sports and physical activities
  • Required teaching eligibility/certification as applicable

Required Skills

  • Knowledge of sports and physical education
  • Teaching and communication skills
  • Leadership and teamwork
  • Fitness and sports skills
  • Patience and discipline
  • Event organization skills
  • Ability to motivate students
