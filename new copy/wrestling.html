Sport Guide

Wrestling

← Back to Sports

Introduction

Wrestling is a combat sport where two athletes compete by grappling, using techniques like holds, throws, and pins to defeat the opponent. It is one of the oldest and most respected Olympic sports.

Importance

  • Builds full-body strength
  • Improves discipline and self-control
  • Enhances stamina and endurance
  • Develops fighting skills and confidence
  • Strong career opportunities in India

Eligibility (Age, Height, Weight)

  • Age: 6–35+ years
  • Height: No strict requirement
  • Weight: Divided into weight categories
  • Fitness: High strength, stamina, and flexibility
  • Training: Requires professional coaching

Beginner to International Career Roadmap

  1. School Level — Join wrestling akhada or academy; learn basic techniques
  2. District Level — Participate in local competitions; improve strength and skills
  3. State Level — Represent district and gain recognition
  4. National Level — Compete in national championships and selection camps
  5. International Level — Represent India
  6. Olympics / World Championships — Compete globally

Training Plan (Beginner to Advanced)

Beginner

  • Basic moves (grip, balance)
  • Bodyweight exercises
  • Flexibility training

Intermediate

  • Technique practice
  • Strength training
  • Sparring sessions

Advanced

  • Advanced techniques
  • Intensive strength & conditioning
  • Match practice and recovery sessions

Diet & Nutrition Plan

Foods: Milk, Ghee, Eggs, Chicken, Dal, Rice, Fruits, Vegetables

Drinks: Milk, Water, Electrolytes

Avoid: Junk food, Soft drinks, Smoking, Alcohol

Required Skills

  • Strength and power
  • Balance
  • Flexibility
  • Technique
  • Mental toughness
  • Discipline

Equipment Used

  • Wrestling mat
  • Singlet (uniform)
  • Headgear
  • Shoes

Competitions

  • School competitions
  • District tournaments
  • State championships
  • National championships
  • Asian Games
  • Olympics

Scholarships

Salary & Income Opportunities

Benefits & Advantages

  • High job opportunities in India
