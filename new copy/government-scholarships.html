Government Sports Scholarships

← Back to Scholarships

Government sports scholarships and athlete-support schemes are financial or developmental programmes provided by the Central Government, State Governments, and their sports departments.

Main objectives

  • Identify talented athletes.
  • Provide financial assistance.
  • Improve coaching and training.
  • Provide sports equipment.
  • Support participation in competitions.
  • Provide sports-science and medical support.
  • Encourage students to continue both education and sports.
  • Help talented athletes progress toward national and international competition.

TOPS

TOPS supports athletes with international training, competitions, coaching, equipment, physiotherapy and other customised support. Core Group athletes receive ₹50,000 monthly OPA; Development Group athletes receive ₹25,000 monthly OPA.

NSDF

The National Sports Development Fund (NSDF) provides flexible assistance for sports development and funds programmes such as TOPS.

How to find scholarships

Check the National Scholarship Portal (NSP), Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports, State Sports Department, State scholarship portal, recognised sports federations, and your school/college scholarship office.

Typical documents

  • Aadhaar/identity document
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Student ID
  • Educational certificates
  • Sports certificates
  • Competition achievement certificates
  • Bank account details
  • Income/caste/domicile certificates where required

Selection

Selection depends on sports performance, competition level, talent, eligibility, academic requirements, and scheme-specific criteria.
