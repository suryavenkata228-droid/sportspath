Chess Career Guidance

Introduction to Chess

← Back to Sports

Chess is a strategic board game played between two players. It focuses on planning, tactics, and intelligence rather than physical strength.

Importance

  • Improves memory and concentration
  • Develops problem-solving skills
  • Enhances strategic thinking
  • Recognized as a competitive sport worldwide
  • Played in international tournaments

Eligibility

  • Age: 5–60+ years
  • Height: Not required
  • Weight: Not required
  • Fitness: Mental fitness more important
  • Training: Coaching helps but self-learning is possible

Training Plan

  • Beginner: Learn rules and moves, practice basic tactics
  • Intermediate: Study openings, solve puzzles
  • Advanced: Game analysis, competitive play, advanced strategies

Diet & Nutrition

  • Healthy balanced diet
  • Brain foods such as nuts and fruits
  • Stay hydrated
